A note on appearance

THE SOURCES BEHIND THE STORY

Evidence &
imagination.

First Humans explores early Homo sapiens. There is no known single first person. The male and female reconstructions are imagined adults, not a known couple. Fossil anatomy informs the story; their exact soft tissues, appearance, personality and clothes are not recoverable.

The modern wardrobe is a playful thought experiment. The lookbook characters and landscapes are artistic interpretations.
